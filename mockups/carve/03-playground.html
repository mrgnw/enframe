Detail measure Energy is the Forward | Gradient switch over the photo, or E. Both pick the cut that shows least, judged differently. Gradient, as caire does, adds up the detail the cut runs through. Forward adds up the new edges that appear once the cut is gone: where the pixels either side close up, and where neighbouring rows shift by different amounts as the seam steps sideways. On Arches at 65%, gradient leaves zigzags along the arch edges; forward keeps them clean but squeezes the middle arch instead. Each finished carve is kept, so switching back is instant.
